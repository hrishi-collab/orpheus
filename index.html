THE ORPHEUS MYTHOLOGY 4-PIECE OUTFIT

We're an independent outfit carving out our own space in the modern rock landscape — four musicians who met in Kolkata's underground scene and stayed for the noise.

OrPhEuS borrows its name from the myth: a musician who walks directly into the dark to bring something back. That is the essence of our record — darker thematic explorations, raw acoustic depths, and cinematic soundscapes built to be played loud, alone, at night.

We write seamlessly in English and Bengali, record in whatever room will have us, and play every show like it's the last one before the lights go out.
